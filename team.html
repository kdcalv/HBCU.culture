Project overview

Why this website was created

HBCU History & Heritage was created to introduce visitors to the history and continuing influence of Historically Black Colleges and Universities.

The website focuses on three main areas: the origins of HBCUs, the people who helped establish them, and the ways these institutions continue to serve students and communities.

Through written content, photographs, video, interactive features, and outside resources, visitors can explore different parts of the HBCU experience.

Our goal Learn.

Make HBCU history easier to explore and understand.
