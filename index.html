Our purpose

Preserving the story. Sharing the impact.

Historically Black Colleges and Universities are an essential part of the American higher education story. Created in response to barriers that excluded Black students, HBCUs built places where education, community, culture, and opportunity could grow together.

This site introduces the history of HBCUs, recognizes people and communities who helped establish them, and highlights the work these institutions continue to do today.

Lee Hall · Florida A&M University

A Florida connection

Excellence on the Hill

Florida A&M University began in 1887 as the State Normal College for Colored Students. From its first class of 15 students, FAMU developed into a public university with programs spanning many fields of study.
