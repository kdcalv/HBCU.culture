HBCUs Today

A continuing tradition of education, innovation, culture, and community leadership.

The present

A legacy that continues to grow

HBCUs remain an important part of American higher education. They offer students opportunities to pursue degrees, participate in research, develop professional skills, and build lifelong relationships.

Today's HBCUs offer programs in areas such as science, technology, engineering, mathematics, business, education, agriculture, health, and the arts.

Beyond academics, HBCUs create communities where students can connect with traditions, organizations, alumni networks, athletics, and leadership opportunities.

Florida A&M University

Located in Tallahassee, Florida, Florida A&M University was founded in 1887. Known as FAMU, the university has a longstanding history of academic achievement, student leadership, and community engagement.
